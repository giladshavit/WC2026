Predicto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Predicto collects the following information when you register: username, email address, and password (stored encrypted). We also collect your prediction data within the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate the Predicto service — managing your account, storing predictions, and displaying leaderboards within leagues you join.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. Predictions become visible to other members of your league after a match kicks off.

We use the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure app performance and understand how users interact with the app (analytics only). Meta may collect device information and app interaction data for these purposes. This is not used to serve advertisements within the app.

For more information: Meta Privacy Policy.

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS 14.5 and later, we request permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. This is used for app analytics purposes only.

  • If you allow: anonymized analytics data may be associated with your device for performance measurement.
  • If you decline: the app works fully — no data is shared with third parties.

You can change this at any time: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Data Storage

Data is stored securely on servers hosted by Railway (railway.app). We retain your data for as long as your account is active.

6. Account Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app via the Settings screen. Alternatively, contact us at giladshavit1@gmail.com.

7. Age

Predicto is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect more data than necessary to operate the service.
